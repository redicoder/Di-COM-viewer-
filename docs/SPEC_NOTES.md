# Spec notes: agreed deviations and open checks

Review of `DISSECTION_TABLE_SPEC.md` before implementation. Each item is either a decision (applied) or a
check that must be done before the related step is closed.

## Decisions

| Spec | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| §2.1 fork UVR into `Assets/ThirdParty/` | Kept as an **embedded package** in `unity/Packages/com.mlavik1.easyvolumerenderer/`, git-URL dependency removed from the manifest, changes logged in its `MODIFICATIONS.md` | Same effect (code in the repo, editable), but assembly definitions and asset GUIDs stay identical to upstream |

| §1 / §3.1 what the student sees (product owner, 2026-10-20) | The app gets two modes: **Scan viewer** (DICOM/NRRD → 3D, as built) and **Anatomy lab**, a body that looks and behaves like a real cadaver. With it off URP copies the real depth after opaques |
| §3.1 the body must look real before anything done to it can (product owner, 2026-10-22) | Three things in the export and one in the shader: each photo's exposure is equalised to its neighbours (the rings round the head were slice-to-slice brightness), the body's outer surface is a 1.5 mm smoothing of the filled outline (the inside stays sharp: sinuses, cut neck), the outermost voxel of tissue takes the colour beneath it (it was skin averaged with the blue gelatin, hence green), and the skin is lit as skin (`LabSkin.hlsl`). | §3.1 the face on the whole body (product owner, 2026-10-22: "still not that good") | On the whole body the head is drawn from the **head export** (0.66 mm) in place of the 1.3 mm data, as a region (`LabRegion`); the skin's colour is averaged over 2 mm of surface (the frozen photographs are blotchy) with the detail in the relief; the living grade kept mild | The face is what a student looks at; 1.3 mm cannot be a face. Regions are the spec's answer to detail on a whole body |
| §3.1 "a 3D game-like body, like a GTA 5 character" (product owner, 2026-10-22) | **This real man, rendered like a game character**, not an artist's idealised body: his skin extracted as a mesh from the photo volume and shaded with a game-quality skin material, graded to a living tone, open living eyes; the volume stays beneath for everything done to him. | §3.1 learn from open projects that made a real-looking body (product owner, 2026-10-22) | Looked at: the **Visible Korean** virtual dissection table (Anatomage: the sectioned photographs as a volume, surface models, labels on both; the same plan as ours), **Z-Anatomy** / BodyParts3D and the Leiden **Open 3D Model** (generic meshes, CC BY-SA: share-alike, and not this man; not used), the open **Unity human skin shader** (separable screen-space scattering; forward-only, Unity 2021), and the **Segmented Internal Organs of the Visible Human Male** (SIO, Höhne et al., UKE Hamburg, **CC BY 4.0**, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.15882018: 202 labelled structures of the torso at 1 mm, made from the same photographs). | §3.1 / §3.2 layers over the labelled body (2026-10-22) | With the SIO labels in place, the lab's first way inside is **layers**: skin, fat, muscle, bone, organ (each object's layer from its name), taken away one at a time over the torso with toolbar buttons; the volume and the skin mesh skip the hidden voxels and the tools treat them as gone, so the muscles and then the organs show in place, in his photographs, with their names under the cursor. | §3.2 layers where there are no labels (head, legs; 2026-10-23) | **Learnt from his own torso**: the SIO gives twenty million voxels with a known layer and a photographed colour; a table over smoothed colour x depth band x local unevenness, filled from them and smoothed across colours, classes every unlabelled tissue voxel as skin, fat, muscle, bone or organ (`tools/classify_tissue.py`), scored on held-out torso slices and cleaned with a 3D vote. The head export gets the same from the body's table, so the face at full detail peels with the body (`LabRegion.ShowLayer`).
